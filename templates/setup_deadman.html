How to enable Telegram

  1. Open Telegram, message @BotFather
  2. Send /newbot, pick a name, copy the token it gives you
  3. Message your new bot once (say "hi")
  4. Visit https://api.telegram.org/bot<TOKEN>/getUpdates in a browser
  5. Copy the chat.id number
  6. In Render → Environment, add:
    TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN=your_token
    TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID=your_chat_id
  7. Redeploy
